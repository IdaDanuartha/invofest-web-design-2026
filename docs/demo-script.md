# FoodLoop - 5-Minute Competition Demo Script

Follow this 10-step walkthrough (matching PRD Section 26) to showcase the end-to-end FoodLoop workflow.

---

## Step 1: Open the Homepage (`#/`)
- Point out the warm editorial aesthetic, clear value proposition (**"Good food deserves another chance."**), and the 4-stage workflow strip (`List → Match → Collect → Make an impact`).
- Highlight the `Demo listing` overlay card on the hero photograph and the live-computed Impact summary further down the page.

## Step 2: Homepage Search & Preview Cards
- In the **Quick Discovery Module**, select **Area:** `Denpasar` and **Category:** `Prepared meals`, then click **Find surplus**.

## Step 3: Filter & Inspect on Discover (`#/discover`)
- Show how the filters on `#/discover` immediately narrow results to **Prepared meals in Denpasar**.
- Toggle between **Grid List** and **Area Map** to show approximate Bali area pins (protecting private addresses until reservation).

## Step 4: Inspect Surplus Detail (`#/food/food_001`)
- Click **View details** on **Vegetable rice bowls** (`food_001`).
- Highlight the **12 of 12 portions available**, the **15:00–17:00 WITA collection window**, the **4°C chilled storage log**, and the explicit allergen disclosure (`Contains soy`).

## Step 5: Reserve 4 Portions as a Recipient
- Ensure the top demo bar is set to **Recipient Org (Community Table)**.
- Set the reservation quantity to `4` portions, check the pickup window confirmation box, and click **Reserve surplus food**.
- Show the **Confirmation panel** with the generated Reservation ID and notice that remaining quantity immediately drops from `12` to `8 portions`.

## Step 6: Switch to Provider Role & View Dashboard (`#/dashboard`)
- Click **Food Provider (Demo Kitchen)** in the top demo bar, then click **Dashboard** (`#/dashboard`).
- Point out the new reservation from `Community Table (Demo)` for `4 portions` of `Vegetable rice bowls` in the **Incoming reservations & handoff queue**.

## Step 7: Confirm Collection & Complete Handoff
- In the reservation row, click **✓ Confirm handoff complete** (or first click **Mark collected** and then **✓ Confirm handoff complete**).
- A toast notification confirms that the handoff is now `COMPLETED` and `4 portions` have been added to Impact metrics.

## Step 8: Verify Updated Metrics on Impact (`#/impact`)
- Navigate to **Our impact** (`#/impact`).
- Show that **Completed handoffs** and **Portions redistributed** have incremented by `1` handoff and `4` portions, and the new handoff appears at the top of the **Recent completed handoffs** table.
- Click **↓ Export handoffs CSV** to demonstrate data portability.

## Step 9: Demonstrate FoodLoop Assist (`#/assistant` & `#/donate`)
- Open **FoodLoop Assist** (`#/assistant`).
- Show how rough provider input (`"12 portions of vegetable rice, prepared at 11:00, chilled, collect before 17:00."`) becomes an editable structured draft with an explicit **Missing information checklist** (never inventing allergen or safety claims).
- Click **Apply draft to Create Listing →** to pre-populate the 3-step stepper at `#/donate`, or switch to **2. Match Surplus** to show transparent match scoring for community kitchens.

## Step 10: Close with the Bali Partner Ecosystem (`#/partners`)
- Open **Partners** (`#/partners`) to show how restaurants, bakeries, grocers, community kitchens, and volunteer scooter relays collaborate across Denpasar and Badung.
